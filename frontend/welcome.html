Key Limitations:
  • Terabytes of unindexed video stored blindly on DVR disks.
  • Manual retrospective review only after accidents or complaints.
  • No GPS telemetry attached directly to road defect frames.
  • Zero automated notifications to municipal engineers.

  • Fleet coordination creates unified multi-bus coverage across cities.
  • Direct dispatch to municipal work orders and traffic command.
