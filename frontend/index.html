WHAT IS URBANSENSE AI?

Reimagining City Sensing Without Expensive Fixed Infrastructure

Traditional smart city initiatives spend millions installing static CCTV cameras with blind spots. UrbanSense AI turns existing civic vehicle fleets into dynamic, mobile sensor nodes.

The Urban Infrastructure Blindspot

Cities across the globe struggle with deteriorating road conditions, invisible bottlenecks, and delayed hazard responses. Municipal authorities rely on manual citizen complaints or slow survey teams that can take months to map defects across thousands of kilometers. By the time an issue is logged, safety hazards and traffic congestion have already multiplied.

🛣️

Autonomous Road Defect Detection

Edge AI devices mounted on public transit buses continuously scan road surfaces for potholes, structural cracking, and waterlogging with sub-meter GPS precision.
