2. Description of Service

UrbanSense AI provides an end-to-end mobile urban sensing and edge intelligence architecture. The platform transforms vehicle fleets—such as municipal buses, maintenance trucks, and transit shuttles—into real-time distributed sensing networks. Our services encompass:

  • Edge AI Video Telemetry & Detection: Real-time computer vision models operating locally on transit vehicles to detect urban infrastructure defects, including potholes, asphalt surface degradation, structural cracks, waterlogging, illegal dumping, and lane blockages.
  • Live GIS Mapping & Asset Visualizations: Leaflet-based geospatial dashboards displaying moving transit units, historical defect heatmaps, road roughness indices, and corridor safety ratings.
  • Civic Command & Work-Order Automation: Tools enabling municipal administrative officers and field operations engineers to assign, track, verify, and resolve physical road maintenance tasks.
  • Traffic & Vehicle Analytics: Anonymized density estimation, corridor transit speed computations, congestion alerts, and automated spatial statistics.
  • Developer APIs & Server-Sent Events (SSE): Programmatic access to real-time events, vehicle coordinates, system health metrics, and audit logs.

You acknowledge that UrbanSense AI is a diagnostic, analytical, and management tool. While our algorithms provide high-confidence insights, the platform does not replace official municipal engineering certification, on-site structural inspections, or statutory civic oversight.

  • Telemetry & Inferred Metadata: By deploying edge sensing nodes on public vehicles, municipal partners grant UrbanSense AI a worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual license to process, store, aggregate, and analyze anonymized road defect coordinates, vibration telemetry, and environmental measurements to train, calibrate, and improve our edge AI models.
